In Practice

What longevity looks like in practice.

We measure the work by where its materials come from, how long its assemblies can be maintained, and whether the landscape remains legible after construction.

82%

Local or reclaimed materials

Material inputs sourced locally or reclaimed, reducing extraction and transport while adding character to every surface.

14

30

Site First

The landscape is not the backdrop. It is part of the structure.

We preserve existing trees, patterns of light, quiet, and movement wherever possible. When concrete is necessary, we use it carefully, with locally informed mixes and a clear understanding of the site it will inhabit.
